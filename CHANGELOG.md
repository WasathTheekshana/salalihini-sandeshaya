## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-10

First public version.

### Added
- All 111 verses of the *Salalihini Sandeshaya* in the original Elu text, each on its own page, with explanations in
  modern Sinhala and English and context notes. Hard verses are marked "General sense".
- A 3D reading experience: a sky, lighting and valley that change with each of the poem's 20 sections, floating
  particles, a flying starling, and film grain.
- A 3D map of the route from Kotte to Kelaniya with landmarks linked to verses, three camera modes and a verse
  scrubber that flies the bird along the path.
- Journey page (the 20 sections), About page (the poem, the poet, the craft, sources) and Developer page with an
  animated character that watches the cursor.
- First-load screen that waits for the real 3D scene, guided-flight mode, keyboard and swipe navigation, and Calm
  mode.
- Sinhala and English typography, SEO metadata for every verse, an app icon and an Apple touch icon.

### Notes
- The Sinhala text comes from Sinhala Wikibooks (originally e-Pothgula, CC BY-SA 4.0) and may contain transcription
  errors; verse 1 is incomplete in that source.
- The explanations are learners' aids and have not been reviewed by a scholar of Elu Sinhala.
