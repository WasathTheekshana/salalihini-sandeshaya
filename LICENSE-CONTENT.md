## Why CC BY-SA 4.0

- The *Salalihini Sandeshaya* itself was composed around 1450 and is in the public domain.
- This repository's digital transcription of it was taken from Sinhala Wikibooks, which republished it from the
  e-Pothgula website under **CC BY-SA 4.0**. That licence is "share-alike", so work built from the transcription
  must be shared under the same licence. For simplicity and compatibility, all the written content here uses it too.
